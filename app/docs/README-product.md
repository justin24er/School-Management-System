# The School Management App

The School Management App is a secure, subscription-based platform that helps
individual primary and secondary schools manage their day-to-day operations:
students, staff, academic records, fees, expenses, and infrastructure
projects, all in one place.

## Who it's for

The School Management App is built for the people who run a school's
internal operations:

- **School Executive Directors**, who need a clear, executive-level view of
  the school's financial and operational health.
- **Head Teachers**, who manage students, teachers, academic performance,
  and infrastructure.
- **Accountants**, who manage fees, payments, expenses, and enrollment
  records.

Version 1 is a school-management tool for these three roles. It is not a
student portal, a parent portal, or a university system. Students, teachers,
and parents do not have individual accounts in this version.

## Getting started

A school starts with a **30-day free trial** — no payment card required.
Starting a trial creates your school's account and signs you in as the
School Executive Director. From there, you can invite your Head Teacher and
Accountant, each with a role suited to their responsibilities.

Every account you invite is scoped to your school only. Schools using The
School Management App are fully separated from one another — nothing about
your school's records, staff, students, or finances is visible to any other
school on the platform.

## What you can do

**As a School Executive Director**, you get a real-time view of revenue,
expenses, resource costs (water, electricity, maintenance), infrastructure
projects, and overall school performance, without needing to dig through
spreadsheets.

**As a Head Teacher**, you manage student records and enrollment status,
record and review academic performance by subject, class, and year, and
track infrastructure projects and resource usage.

**As an Accountant**, you manage student fees and payments, record and
categorize expenses, and handle enrollment and staff administrative records.
Every payment and financial correction is recorded with a clear history —
nothing is silently overwritten.

## Subscriptions and renewal

After your 30-day trial, continued access requires an active subscription.
The platform uses a service voucher system: your account representative can
provide you with a voucher code for a given service period (for example, 3,
6, or 12 months), which you redeem to extend your school's access.

If a subscription lapses, your school enters a short grace period before
operational features are paused. Your data is never deleted when a
subscription lapses — it's simply held safely until you renew.

## Security and privacy

Your account is protected with:

- Passwords that are never stored in plain text, using modern, adaptive
  password hashing.
- Secure, server-managed sessions.
- Protection against repeated automated login attempts.
- Optional two-factor authentication for your account.
- Strict separation between your school's data and every other school's
  data on the platform.

We follow the principle of least privilege throughout the platform: every
role sees only the information relevant to its responsibilities.

## System requirements

The School Management App works from any modern web browser on desktop,
laptop, tablet, or mobile. No local installation is required.

## Support

If you run into an issue or have a question about your account or
subscription, contact your account representative or the support channel
provided when you started your trial.
